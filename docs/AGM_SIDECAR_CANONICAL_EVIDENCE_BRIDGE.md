# Sidecar to canonical evidence bridge

Sidecar packages are transport and inspection material, not an alternative
project-requirement state. A satisfiable artifact carries explicit
`obligation_refs` plus case/contribution/policy bindings, commits, content
digest, producer declaration, source tool, time and media type.

Only a declared compatibility mapping is accepted: test results may support
test requirements, activity supports Agent-scope, impact statements support
impact requirements, and declarations/supporting statements support their
textual requirements. Diffs are inspection-only; receipts and sidecar
human-confirmation objects never replace legal human confirmation. Unknown,
uncompiled or incompatible references are rejected.

The bridge receipt contains package, artifact and canonical evidence IDs plus
an audit record. Digest verification proves stored content integrity and
case/policy/contribution binding; it does not prove a claimed producer is a
real platform identity or that a person performed an action.
