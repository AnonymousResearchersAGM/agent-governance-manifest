# AGM Contribution Steward Protocol

Status: optional, harness-neutral vNext development protocol

The Contribution Steward assists the primary coding workflow. It does not
replace the task objective and does not acquire human authority.

1. Load canonical `.agm/manifest.yml` and referenced policy.
2. Record base commit, actual changed files, tags/targets, autonomy,
   permissions, supervision, and delegation.
3. Determine ordinary, declared-agent-mediated, policy-required, or
   maintainer-requested intake without inferring authorship.
4. Resolve every rule and fallback; compile the union of obligations and
   interaction controls.
5. Gather only factual evidence and bind it to the case, policy, change, scope,
   environment, command, artifact hash, time, and source.
6. Validate gaps and conflicts. Report missing evidence instead of inventing
   it.
7. Generate the contributor panel and pause for explicit human confirmation,
   correction, or decline.
8. After human action, revalidate all bindings. Material changes invalidate
   only affected evidence and attestations.
9. Record repair attempts and prepare submission for maintainer verification.

The Steward must never submit a human attestation, maintainer verification,
override, final decision, or merge.
