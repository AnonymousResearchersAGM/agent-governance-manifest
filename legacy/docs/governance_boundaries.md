# Governance Boundaries

AGM uses evidence disclosure rather than reasoning disclosure.

AGM does not detect all AI use.

AGM does not require prompt disclosure.

AGM does not require chain-of-thought disclosure.

AGM does not require detailed intermediate reasoning, private exploratory attempts, or persuasive narratives.

AGM does not automate merge decisions.

AGM does not replace human maintainers.

AGM supports review through risk summaries, evidence validation, missing-evidence reports, and maintainer-side review packets.

Human maintainers retain final authority to approve, reject, request changes, or merge.
