# AGM Maintainer Review Checklist

- [ ] Read `agent_governance_manifest.yml`.
- [ ] Independently classify changed files into risk zones.
- [ ] Confirm the highest risk level.
- [ ] Compare required evidence against submitted evidence.
- [ ] Review contribution report for affected files, risk declaration, AI disclosure, limitations, and linked issues.
- [ ] Review test report for commands, outcomes, failures, and coverage notes.
- [ ] Review trace manifest for provenance and evidence file references.
- [ ] For high or critical changes, check human review declaration.
- [ ] Consider linked issue context and issue-related hints.
- [ ] Record reviewer attention points.
- [ ] Leave final approve, reject, or merge decision to the human maintainer.
