# AGM Human Review Declaration

## Structured Fields

- human_confirmation_required: "<true|false>"
- human_confirmation_status: "<required pending|required completed|not required>"
- reviewer_name_or_role: "<name, handle, or maintainer role>"
- reviewed_risk_zones:
  - "<risk zone id>"
- reviewed_evidence_files:
  - "<evidence file>"
- decision_scope: "Human review confirmation only; final merge decision remains separate."
- notes: "<short factual notes or none>"

## Boundary

This declaration confirms human review status for higher-risk changes. It does not require disclosure of contributor prompts, private reasoning, or exploratory work.
