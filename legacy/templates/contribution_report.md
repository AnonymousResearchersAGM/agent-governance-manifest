# AGM Contribution Report

## Structured Fields

- contribution_summary: "<short factual summary of the change>"
- affected_files:
  - "<path>"
- declared_risk_zone:
  - "<risk zone id>"
- declared_risk_level: "<low|medium|high|critical>"
- ai_assistance_disclosure: "<brief disclosure of AI assistance, without prompts or private reasoning>"
- tests_run:
  - "<command or not run>"
- test_outcomes: "<pass|fail|not run, with concise result>"
- known_limitations:
  - "<limitation or none>"
- linked_issues:
  - "<issue id/url or none>"
- human_confirmation_status: "<not required|required pending|required completed>"
- missing_evidence:
  - "<missing evidence item or none>"

## Notes

This report records independently checkable evidence. It does not request or include original prompts, chain-of-thought, detailed intermediate reasoning, private exploratory attempts, or persuasive narratives about why the change should be accepted.
